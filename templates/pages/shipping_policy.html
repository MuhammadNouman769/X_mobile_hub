Shipping Policy

Delivery Timeframes

Orders are typically processed within 24 hours and delivered within 2-4 business days for major cities, and 4-7 business days for other locations across Pakistan.

Shipping Charges

We offer free delivery on all orders above {{ FREE_SHIPPING_THRESHOLD|currency }}. Orders below this amount incur a flat shipping fee, calculated automatically at checkout.

Order Tracking

Once your order is shipped, you will receive a confirmation with tracking details via email and SMS. You can also view your order status anytime from your account's order history page.

Delivery Attempts

Our courier partners will attempt delivery up to 2 times. If delivery is unsuccessful, the order will be returned to our warehouse and you will be contacted to arrange redelivery.

Cash on Delivery

Cash on Delivery is available nationwide. Please have the exact amount ready at the time of delivery.
