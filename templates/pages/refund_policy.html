Refund & Return Policy

7-Day Return Window

If you're not satisfied with your purchase, you may request a return within 7 days of delivery, provided the product is unused, in its original packaging, and includes all accessories and documentation.

Non-Returnable Situations

  • Physical damage or liquid damage caused after delivery
  • Missing original box, accessories, or invoice
  • Products with a broken warranty seal (unless defective on arrival)

Refund Process

Once we receive and inspect your returned item, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund. Approved refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to your original payment method, or as store credit for Cash on Delivery orders.

Exchanges

If your device arrives with a manufacturing defect, we offer a free exchange for the same model, subject to stock availability.

How to Request a Return

Contact our support team via the Contact Us page with your order number and reason for return, and we'll guide you through the next steps.
